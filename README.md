# Car Escape: Infinite Pursuit

**A free online car chase and drifting game** built with HTML5 Canvas, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript.

[**▶ Play Car Escape now**](https://serakib.github.io/GreenLine/)

Escape pursuing police cars, master your steering and drift controls, collect golden coins for bonus points, and push your high score through 900+ increasingly challenging levels. Play directly in a modern desktop or mobile browser—no installation required.

## Game features

- **Endless progression:** advance through 900+ levels with smoothly increasing difficulty.
- **Police pursuit:** stay ahead of the pursuing police cars and survive for as long as possible.
- **Drifting and driving:** use steering, acceleration, reverse and drift controls.
- **Golden coin bonuses:** collect coins to earn extra points.
- **Responsive controls:** keyboard and on-screen touch controls for desktop and mobile.
- **High-score leaderboard:** keep track of your best runs locally.
- **Game effects:** dynamic camera movement, particles and sound effects.

## How to play

1. Open the [game](https://serakib.github.io/GreenLine/).
2. Press **গেম শুরু করুন** to begin.
3. Use the on-screen controls or keyboard controls to steer, accelerate, reverse and drift.
4. Avoid the police, collect coins and reach each level's score target.
